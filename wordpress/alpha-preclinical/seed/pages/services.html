Preclinical in vivo contract research

Alpha specializes in early preclinical research, with a focus on in vivo PK/PD lead optimization, feasibility proof of concept, efficacy and iterative preclinical testing.

We take pride in humane animal care and high-quality research, delivered on a timely schedule at a cost that works for the research community. Every client works directly with the scientists running their study.

  • PK/PD and lead optimizationExposure, dose selection and pharmacodynamic readouts to rank candidates and set efficacy doses.
  • Feasibility and proof of conceptFirst in vivo tests of a mechanism, designed to give a clear go or no-go answer.
  • Efficacy studiesValidated disease models with predefined endpoints, controls and group sizes.
  • Iterative testingRepeat rounds as your molecule evolves, run the same way each time so results compare.
