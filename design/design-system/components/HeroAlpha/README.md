# HeroAlpha

The homepage "wow": the alpha from the logo, traced and enlarged, with five strata bands rising into it and drifting.

- Lives only in the homepage hero, right column (max 400px wide), on `abyss`.
- Fill: `mist` ground, then five bands from top to bottom `shallows`, `strata-2`, `current`, `strata-4`, `tide`; a 0.6 `shallows` hairline at 70% traces the edge.
- Motion: the bands rise 78px into the letter on arrival (2.8s), drift sideways at different speeds, and fill further as the visitor scrolls. Paused by the hero's Pause button and frozen under reduced motion.
- The shape is the logo's own alpha (`assets/Logos/alpha-strata.svg`): never redraw, recolour outside these tokens, or stretch it.
