# CTABand

The closing band on every page: an `abyss` field, centred heading, one sentence and the quote button, framed by contour lines.

- The only centred text in the system. Heading Bricolage 600, 38-64px, max 21ch; the sentence in `ink-on-abyss` 19px.
- Primary: `ap-btn-light`; optional secondary: `ap-btn-ghost-dark`.
- Contour lines in `shallows` at 35% draw in when the band arrives.
- Wording varies by page ("Put this team on your program", "Want the authors on your study?") but always leads to the contact form. The reply-time promise in the example is placeholder copy: use only what Alpha confirms.
