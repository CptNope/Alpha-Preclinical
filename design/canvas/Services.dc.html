Preclinical in vivo research, with every support service under one roof

From formulation and dosing to surgery, imaging and laboratory analysis, the work behind your study happens in our Worcester facility, run by the people who designed it.

Preclinical in vivo contract research

Alpha specializes in early preclinical research, with a focus on in vivo PK/PD lead optimization, feasibility proof of concept, efficacy and iterative preclinical testing.

We take pride in humane animal care and high-quality research, delivered on a timely schedule at a cost that works for the research community. Every client works directly with the scientists running their study.

  • PK/PD and lead optimization

    Exposure, dose selection and pharmacodynamic readouts to rank candidates and set efficacy doses.

  • Feasibility and proof of concept

    First in vivo tests of a mechanism, designed to give a clear go or no-go answer.

  • Efficacy studies

    Validated disease models with predefined endpoints, controls and group sizes.

  • Iterative testing

    Repeat rounds as your molecule evolves, run the same way each time so results compare.
