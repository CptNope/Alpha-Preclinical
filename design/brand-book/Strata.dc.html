Alpha Preclinical brand book06 · Graphic elements

The strata motif

Full strata. Five bands, deepest at the top, the last melting into the next ground. Once per piece: a hero, a cover, a banner.

Contour line. One 1.5 px current line: the strata reduced to a divider. Use between sections instead of boxes.

Wave-cut edge. A photo may take one wave edge, bottom or right. Never more than one per section.

Corners and shapes

14 px radius on photos and panels, 6 px on inputs, full pills for buttons and chips. Pills are only ever actions.

Never

Gradients between the blues, glows, outlines on bands, bands in non-brand colours, or more than one strata moment on a page.
