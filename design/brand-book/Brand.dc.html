Who we are and what the brand is for

Alpha Preclinical is a preclinical in vivo contract research organization in Worcester, Massachusetts, founded in 2020. Scientists with published research run PK/PD, efficacy and proof-of-concept studies for biotech and pharma teams.

Everything the brand does has one job: make a scientist trust Alpha enough to request a study quote. We earn that with specifics (the actual model, assay or instrument), real people and real data, not adjectives.

The one rule that never bends

The logo and its blues are on the building signage. They are never redrawn, recoloured or "refreshed".

The idea: strata

The alpha in the logo is filled with stacked bands of blue, deep to pale. That layering is the brand's one signature shape. In every medium it appears as flat wave bands in the brand blues, or reduced to a single contour line.

  • Flat bands that overlap, like the logo: no gradients, glows or purple.
  • Full strata once per piece; elsewhere, one contour line.
  • On screens the bands drift slowly, and a visitor can pause them.
