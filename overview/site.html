WordPress build

The production site is a WordPress 6.9+ block theme in wordpress/alpha-preclinical/:

  • Every bespoke component is a custom block whose content can be bound to data (Block Bindings API).
  • Publications, team members and open roles are their own content types; site details and quote routing are in one settings page.
  • The approved content imports in one step (wp alpha seed), and old Wix URLs and PDF links redirect.

On staging, Alpha's team logs in and answers the open questions on a Content review page; many answers update the staging site as soon as they are saved.

Details and setup: theme README. Next step: staging on Cloudways for the client to review.
