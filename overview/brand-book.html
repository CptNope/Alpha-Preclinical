The book

Eight pages at US Letter landscape, exported as one PDF for the client. Each page is shown at full detail; widen the window to read it larger.

Business cards

Trim size 3.5 × 2 in. The artwork includes a 0.125 in bleed on every side; keep text 0.25 in inside the trim. Order on 16 pt matte stock or heavier. The email and direct phone are placeholders until each person confirms them.

Letterhead

For letters and quotes. The strata band appears on the first page only; continuation pages carry the logo and page number.

Email signature

Download the signature HTML

Open the file in a browser, replace the [bracketed] details, select the signature and paste it into Gmail (Settings › Signature), Outlook (Settings › Mail › Compose and reply) or Apple Mail. It uses Arial because email apps don't load web fonts, and loads the logo from the live site.

LinkedIn banners

LinkedIn places the logo or profile photo over the lower-left corner, so text sits to the right.

LinkedIn posts

One post per new paper, blog post or open role. Use real titles and the author's real photo; keep one idea per image.

Editing and files

  • design/brand-book/*.dc.html: every page and template above, editable on a Claude design canvas (one file per artboard; canvas.json lays them out)
  • design/brand-book/email-signature.html: the paste-ready signature
  • Logo: only 240 px PNGs exist. Ask Alpha for vector originals (AI, EPS or SVG) before anything is printed.
  • Print colour: take CMYK and Pantone values from the sign maker's spec rather than converting the screen colours.
  • Recommendations awaiting client sign-off: minimum logo size, colour proportions, line-icon style (see Decisions).
