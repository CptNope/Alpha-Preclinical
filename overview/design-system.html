Principles

Alpha Preclinical LLC is a preclinical in vivo contract research organization (CRO) at 722 Plantation Street, Worcester, Massachusetts, founded in 2020. Its clients are biotech and pharma scientists who need PK/PD, efficacy and proof-of-concept studies run carefully, quickly and at a fair cost. The website's one job is to make a scientist trust Alpha enough to request a study quote.

This is the client-approved system (November 2026 revision, main of CptNope/Alpha-Preclinical). It keeps the brand's existing colours and logo exactly, because they are on the building signage, and gives them a sharper, more confident web presence. It is the reference for the production WordPress FSE block theme.

Hard constraint: never change the brand colours or the logo. Every blue in this system was sampled from the logo and the signage.

The idea: strata

The alpha in the logo is filled with stacked bands of blue, from deep abyss to pale shallows. That layering is the brand's one signature shape. On the web it becomes strata: flowing wave bands in the brand blues.

  • Homepage hero: the logo's own alpha, traced and enlarged (HeroAlpha). Five strata bands rise into it on arrival, drift, and fill it further as the visitor scrolls. Full-height drifting waves run along the foot of the hero.
  • Every inner page: a shorter drifting band at the foot of the header (StrataBand), and a thin one under blog post headers. Each carries a Pause animation button.
  • Between sections: the motif shrinks to a single 1.5px current contour line (ContourDivider), or contour lines framing a CTA band.
  • Photos may take one wave-cut edge (bottom or right), never more than one per section.
  • No gradients, glows or purple. Depth comes from flat bands overlapping, the way the logo does it.

The site matches the sign visitors see at the entrance.

Spacing, radius and shadow

  • space-1 8px · Icon-to-label gap.
  • space-2 16px · Mobile gutter; gap inside lists.
  • space-3 24px · Paragraph and block gap.
  • space-4 40px · Desktop gutter; gap between columns.
  • space-5 72px · Gap between a heading column and its content; mobile section padding.
  • space-6 128px · Vertical section padding on desktop (area sections use 120px).
  • radius-s 6px · Inputs, the skip link, photo labels.
  • radius-m 14px · Photos, panels, team faces, the 'Led by' chip, dropdown panels.
  • radius-pill 999px · Buttons, filter chips, the motion toggle, icon circles. The only pill shape in the system.
  • shadow-lift · Header once scrolled; card hover lift. Pointer devices only.
  • shadow-menu · Open dropdown panels only. Floating menus are the one place a heavier shadow is used.

HeroAlpha

The homepage "wow": the alpha from the logo, traced and enlarged, with five strata bands rising into it and drifting.

  • Lives only in the homepage hero, right column (max 400px wide), on abyss.
  • Fill: mist ground, then five bands from top to bottom shallows, strata-2, current, strata-4, tide; a 0.6 shallows hairline at 70% traces the edge.
  • Motion: the bands rise 78px into the letter on arrival (2.8s), drift sideways at different speeds, and fill further as the visitor scrolls. Paused by the hero's Pause button and frozen under reduced motion.
  • The shape is the logo's own alpha (assets/Logos/alpha-strata.svg): never redraw, recolour outside these tokens, or stretch it.

StrataBand

Expertise

Six therapeutic areas, one team.

The signature: wave bands in the brand blues, drifting slowly at the foot of every page header.

  • Inner pages: a 110px band of four waves (tide, current, shallows, then paper so it melts into the page). Blog posts carry a thinner one under the header. The homepage hero uses the full-height version with five bands and the HeroAlpha.
  • Each wave is a repeating path twice the viewport wide that slides left at its own speed (30-46s, alternate directions), so the layers drift against each other.
  • Always ship the Pause animation button (WCAG 2.2.2). Under prefers-reduced-motion the waves start paused; with JavaScript off they rest still.
  • Flat fills only: no gradients, glows or purple. Depth comes from bands overlapping, as in the logo.
