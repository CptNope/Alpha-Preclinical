A preclinical partner built by people who've run the studies

Alpha Preclinical is a US-owned discovery research company in Worcester, Massachusetts. We were founded in 2020 by scientists and technicians who spent decades in pharma, biotech and contract research.

Why we started Alpha

Drug developers are under pressure to reach the clinic faster and spend less getting there. Outsourcing preclinical work should help, but too often it means handing a program to a queue.

We built Alpha to work differently. Every client gets a personal, scientist-to-scientist relationship: we help design the study, run it carefully, and tell you plainly what we're seeing as it happens.

Our goal is to be a strategic partner to the teams we work with, delivering well-executed, cost-effective studies on a timeline that keeps their drug development and research programs moving.

What working with us is like

Trust

Trust is how our team works together and how we build long-term relationships with clients. We earn it through honest, open conversations about a shared goal: a successful program.

Innovation

Our scientists bring decades of experience across every part of preclinical research, and we apply it to new modalities and models, not just familiar ones.

Transparency

You hear about operational and study details clearly and promptly, including the unexpected ones. Transparency is what makes trust possible.

Humane animal care

High-quality data starts with well-cared-for animals. Our team is committed to humane care in every study we run.
